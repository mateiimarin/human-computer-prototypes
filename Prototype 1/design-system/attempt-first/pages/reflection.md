# Session reflection

Read `../MASTER.md` first. Reuse contribution tokens and evidence components from the workspace.

**Student's question:** What did I contribute, and what should I revisit?

## Composition

Centered 760 px maximum column with heading “Who did the thinking?” Supporting copy: “A record of visible contributions and help in this task.” Use a compact task identity row beside the heading; keep the evidence list as the focus.

Show an ordered list of attempt steps and assistance events. Each entry includes the actual step title, one explicit origin label, and an evidence disclosure linking to the original work or tutor exchange. Origin options: You introduced, AI prompted, AI supplied, Mixed, Unclear. Entries can wrap across several lines; no badge truncation.

Do not convert the list into percentages, a pie chart, a progress ring, a competitive “You vs AI” view, or an assessment of internal understanding. Include a short factual note: “Contribution origins do not measure understanding.” Use the same evidence in the workspace and reflection.

Each entry has a “Flag attribution” action. In the prototype this can open a small Dialog with a reason field and Flag / Cancel; submitting records a local visible “Flagged for correction” note. It does not contact a teacher or external service. Preserve the original label and mark it disputed, unless a complete explicit correction flow is separately designed.

## Gaps and exit

If gaps remain, show them in a persistent ochre section before completion actions: “Still to revisit,” with step references and a Return to revise action. Do not show a resolved badge on this path.

If no gaps remain, state the review outcome precisely. In both cases, the main action is “Finish session,” with “Reopen this task” secondary. Finishing may show a small summary and offer “Choose another task”; opening another task follows the discard choice if it will clear the current temporary session.

No optional quiz, course graph, daily streak, reward, or global history controls unless the team adds their complete extension paths. Finishing is available without self-testing.

## Check

Every entry links to evidence actually displayed in this scenario. Unclear/mixed and disputed attribution have understandable text states. Finishing with gaps preserves those gaps in the visible summary and never implies mastery.
